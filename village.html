THE SITUATION

A village of 18 faces an enemy host. Scout its movements, gather supplies, fortify, recruit, negotiate, or risk an attack. Each accepted commander action advances one day and consumes food equal to one for every six villagers, rounded up. The state persists across controller restarts.

Initial supplies: 24 food, 10 wood, 6 stone, 3 defenders; defense 2, morale 65. The enemy begins at strength 8 with 10 supply. The live world may have moved on: ask !status in chat.

JAY COMMANDS

Only messages from BigRigJay's numeric Twitch chatter ID issue commander actions. Type these in normal channel chat:

CommandWhat it attempts
!do scout northLook for enemy pressure. East, south, and west also work.
!do gather foodGain 8 food. Gathering wood gains 5; stone gains 4.
!do build palisadeSpend 5 wood and 2 stone for 2 defense.
!do recruit defendersSpend 4 food for 2 defenders.
!do defend westPrepare a defense; choose a direction or type !do defend for general defense.
!do attack eastSend defenders toward a direction.
!do negotiateSpend 3 food to stop a raid that turn.
!status / !helpAsk for the live world or a command reminder.

The enemy's front starts hidden. Scouting or a raid may reveal it. Gemma can raid (1 supply), fortify (+1 strength for 2 supply), recruit (+2 strength for 3 supply), or wait. An unaffordable move becomes a quiet turn. Reduce enemy strength to zero to win; population or morale reaching zero loses the game.

CHAT COUNCIL

Viewers can type !suggest fortify west, !suggest gather food, or any short proposal. Suggestions enter Gemma's recent council context; they do not spend resources or advance the day. Viewers can also use !status and !help.

The explicit commands are the dependable path tonight. Results are decided by the world rules before the bot narrates them. A clever sentence in chat cannot invent a victory.
